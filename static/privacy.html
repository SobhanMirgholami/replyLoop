Privacy policy

replyloop is a private tool that answers comments and messages on the Instagram accounts of its owner.

What we process

When someone comments on one of our posts or messages our account, we receive their Instagram username, an Instagram-scoped ID, the comment or message text, and whether they follow our account. We use this only to reply to them automatically.

How long we keep it

Comment and reply logs are kept so we can see what the tool did. Message IDs used to prevent duplicate replies are deleted after 3 days. We never sell or share this data. If AI replies are turned on, the comment text is sent to Anthropic to write a reply.
